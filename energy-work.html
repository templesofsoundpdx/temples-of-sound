Energy work through sacred sound

Experience singing meditation with energy work in Portland, Oregon. Temples of Sound combines vocal toning, humming, breath, and the seven-chakra system to explore awareness, embodiment, and the movement from Source into individual experience.

The Energy Work

The meditation takes us on a journey before embodiment, beginning as awareness before form. From there, we move through the seven-chakra system as a framework for coming into human experience.

Each chakra represents another layer of awareness becoming available: arrival, feeling, personal power, compassion, authenticity, intuition, and expanded awareness. We work with the traditional chakra colors — red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and violet — along with the associated octave tones, imagery, and Bija mantras to give each stage of the journey something that can be seen, felt, sounded, and experienced.

The practice is not meant as a literal biological account of development, but as an energetic exploration of awareness moving toward embodiment.

As the journey unfolds, awareness shifts from Source into an individual perspective — this particular body, voice, life, and experience — while still remaining connected to the larger awareness from which it arose.
