Shruti Box Replacement Levers

We are developing durable replacement note selector tabs for a variety of shruti box sizes, styles and manufacturers.

Our proposed offerings include:

  • Individual replacement levers
  • Complete replacement sets
  • Custom-sized levers
  • Traditional and custom color options
  • Resin, wood and other material options, including resin with preserved flowers and plants

We are exploring custom sizing based on photographs and measurements of original levers.

Currently in product development. Pricing, fit and materials are not yet finalized. No orders or payments are being accepted. We welcome inquiries from shruti box owners interested in replacement parts.
